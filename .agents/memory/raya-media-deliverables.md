---
name: RAYA media deliverables
description: Why animated product media should remain separate from the operational portal
---

Create animated RAYA product demos as dedicated video artifacts rather than routes inside the customs portal.

**Why:** The video artifact supplies the recording lifecycle, export-safe audio, and iframe-only scene controls while keeping marketing media out of production portal navigation.

**How to apply:** For future RAYA videos, reuse or extend the dedicated video artifact unless the user explicitly asks for an in-app media page.
